    Hold the field

    1. Press Play. Each day uses real NASA satellite readings for this farm.
    2. Watch the Scout. Heavy rain in the hills upstream means a flash flood is coming.
    3. Act in time. Raise the bund or harvest early. Save Rahim's rice.

    Data & model

    This is a simplified model, calibrated to reproduce one real flood. It is a learning game, not a forecast.

    • Water level is a simple bucket: upstream rain (arriving after a short delay) and farm rain fill it; drainage empties it.
    • The flood starts when water rises above the bund. The crop dies after a few days fully under water.
    • The Scout warns using the 3-day total of upstream rain.
    • Rice matures linearly from transplanting. You can harvest from 80% maturity; yield equals maturity.
