HOLD THE FIELD 1.0.0
NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 - Field Shift

NEW IN 1.0
- Full voxel world: walk, jump and fly around Rahim's farm in the Sunamganj haor.
- Satellite Scout dashboard with interactive charts (E key or the spyglass).
- Raise the bund with mud bricks, harvest with the sickle, watch the flood arrive.
- Worlds are saved automatically. Replay a season or watch "Rahim's way".
- Advancements, a season report and a chart against the farmer who got no warning.
- Rebindable keys, video and sound options, F3 debug screen, F2 screenshots.

REAL NASA DATA
The game ships with SAMPLE data so it always starts. Open the "NASA Data" tab and press
"Update NASA data" to download the real 2017 season from NASA POWER. You need an internet
connection once; after that the game works offline.

TIPS
- Rain in the Meghalaya hills reaches the haor about two days later.
- Short-duration rice ripens sooner. You can harvest from 80% maturity.
- Double-tap Space to fly up and see over the bund.
